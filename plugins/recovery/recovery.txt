Controller-prompt fragments owned by the recovery plugin (see plugins/prompt_text.py for
the [section] convention; {placeholders} are filled by plugin.py via str.replace).
The note_* sections are the per-event prompt notes the runner carries between steps;
a note expires when the stage completes or a later close measurably HOLDS.

[context]
Recovery: {note}

[note_empty_grasp]
Empty close; do not retry on an edge/corner. Recenter body and confirm depth.

[note_unverified_grasp]
Grasp not verified; continue GRASP until width and images show a real hold.

[note_lost_grasp]
Grasp lost; return to GRASP, recenter the object body, then confirm depth.

[note_unsettled]
Closed width is unsettled; wait before moving.
