Controller-prompt fragments owned by the affordance tool: the AFFORD field templates
that bind the planner's contact part to the drawn dot (see plugins/prompt_text.py for
the [section] convention; {placeholders} are filled by plugin.py via str.replace).
The pointing/verify ROLE prompts live in affordance_point.txt / affordance_verify.txt.

[with_wrist]
{part} = {color_name} dot in the {wrist_view} (also in {view_name})

[front_only]
{part} = {color_name} dot in {view_name}

[cross_view_ref]
A SECOND reference image follows: {view_name} with the {color_name} dot already committed on the SAME physical spot. Your point must mark that SAME physical spot as seen in the FIRST image -- use the reference to disambiguate look-alikes.
